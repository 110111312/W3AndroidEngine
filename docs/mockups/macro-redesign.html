Macro panel — redesign sketch

760 × 360 dp (your phone), real game art from your 1.29.2 data, drawn over a mission screenshot. Click things: a button opens its sheet, a tab switches, an available icon adds a ticket, a ticket takes one tap to come sooner and a hold to go later, the × removes it. On a phone, pinch to zoom the frame.

What changed, and why

  • Buttons 44 dp, art inside, count as a badge. One visual idea instead of a number and a word: the icon says what it is, the badge says how many tickets are behind it. Two labels of 10 px at the bottom of the screen were the least readable thing in the old layout.
  • The badge hides at 0 instead of showing “0”, and reads blank while the plan has not seeded yet.
  • Three tabs, not two. Upgrades had nowhere to live, so they sat inside the build sheet and every comparison looked wrong: a Moon Well and Strength of the Moon are not things you choose between.
  • The queue is a list with words. Today a red frame is the only sign that a ticket is stuck; the row says needs Ancient of Wind in the same breath as the name, and the frame colour stays for a glance.
  • Numbers start at 1. They are positions in a queue, not array indices.
  • Handles you can see. A queue row has the × on its right; the whole row is the “sooner” target and a hold is “later”, so no 15 dp chip has to carry both the number and a second meaning.
  • 44 dp art hits the finger. Every icon is at least 44 dp in both variants, the minimum for a thumb that is not looking.

Open questions for you

  1. A or B? A shows the whole plan without scrolling and covers the fight; B keeps the game visible but halves the icons on screen.
  2. Is 3 tabs right, or do upgrades belong under BUILD as a second row?
  3. Should the queue show the gold it is about to spend (a total under the list)?
  4. The build icon is the Night Elf hammer; the army icon is a Huntress face. Pick a better pair?
  5. Do you want the hold-to-go-later gesture at all, or two arrows per row?
